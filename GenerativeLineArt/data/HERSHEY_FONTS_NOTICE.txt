The single-line fonts in this folder (futural.jhf, timesr.jhf, scripts.jhf)
are Hershey fonts, taken from https://github.com/kamalmostafa/hershey-fonts.

The Hershey Fonts were originally created by Dr. A. V. Hershey while working
at the U. S. National Bureau of Standards.
